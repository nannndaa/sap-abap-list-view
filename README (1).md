## Overall Learning 

>During my SAP ABAP internship, I gained hands-on experience in SAP application development and learned the fundamentals of the ABAP programming language. I worked with the ABAP Workbench to create classical, interactive, and ALV reports, developed user-friendly screens using Module Pool Programming, and designed business documents using Smart Forms. I learned to work with the ABAP Dictionary, internal tables, function modules, and BAPIs, while also gaining practical experience in Batch Data Communication (BDC) through SHDB recordings and Excel data uploads. Additionally, I explored SAP enhancements such as Customer Exits, BAdIs, Menu Exits, and Screen Exits to customize standard SAP functionality. Throughout the internship, I worked with standard SAP tables like VBAK, VBAP, LIKP, LIPS, and MSEG, practiced debugging and testing techniques, and developed a better understanding of real-world SAP business processes. Overall, the internship strengthened my technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills and provided a solid foundation for a career as an SAP ABAP developer.
